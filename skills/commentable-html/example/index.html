Augmentation is worth 4.1 points, the most of the three parts

  • Without augmentation, accuracy falls from 74.0 to 69.9, a drop of 4.1 points.
  • Pretraining is second at 2.5 points, and the new loss is last at 0.9.
  • Every drop is larger than the spread between seeds, which is 0.5 points at most.
